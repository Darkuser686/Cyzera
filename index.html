About us

A modern cyber identity,
built by students.

CYZERA represents a bold and futuristic vision in the field of cyber security and digital innovation.

The name symbolizes a modern cyber identity that combines advanced technology, intelligent security practices, and digital resilience to meet the challenges of the rapidly evolving cyber world.

The initiative also serves as a platform for students to showcase creativity, technical talent, and problem-solving abilities through competitions, hackathons, technical talks, and collaborative projects. By bridging academic learning with practical implementation, CYZERA helps students become industry-ready professionals capable of contributing to the cyber security ecosystem.

What holds it together is a simple belief: security is a skill you practise, not a subject you memorise. So the club is deliberately hands-on. Members decode ciphers, sit inside escape rooms, generate with AI, shoot and edit, argue over chess boards, and take apart the way systems fail — then present what they learned to the next batch coming up behind them.

CYZERA is open to every student on campus, from every branch and every year. No prior background in security is expected — curiosity is the only entry requirement, and the club is designed so a first-year with no experience and a final-year with a portfolio both find something worth their time.

Our events

Three flagships. Eleven ways in.

Every CYZERA event is designed so that a complete beginner can enter and still walk out having learned something they can use.

EVENT 01

Shield X Tech

4 tracks Solo & team

CYZERA's flagship technical showcase, built as four parallel tracks so that problem-solvers, creators and competitive gamers all have somewhere to go. The name is the idea: a shield — defence and security thinking — crossed with tech — the tools students are already curious about.

Participants can enter a single track or several. Tracks are scheduled so entries do not collide, and results across all four feed a combined leaderboard for the day.

Treasure Hunt

A chained clue-trail across campus. Each clue is a small puzzle — ciphers, QR codes, riddles and logic steps — and solving it points to where the next one is hidden. Fastest team to the final clue wins.

AI Image Generation

A prompt-craft contest. The theme is revealed on the spot and entries are judged on how well the image reads against the brief, the quality of the prompting, and originality of the concept.

AI Video

Short-form video built with AI tools. Judged on narrative, coherence between shots, edit quality and how convincingly the idea lands inside a tight runtime.

eSports

Competitive gaming run as a seeded knockout bracket, with fixtures published before play begins so every team knows its path to the final.

EVENT 02

Cyber Escape Room

Timed Teams of 3–5

An escape room where every lock is a security problem. A team is placed in a themed room with a countdown running, and the only way forward is to solve a chain of challenges — each solution producing the code, key or password that opens the next stage.

The puzzle chain is drawn from genuine security fundamentals rather than invented trivia: classical ciphers such as Caesar and Vigenère, Base64 and hex decoding, steganography with data hidden inside images and audio, log and metadata analysis, weak-password reasoning, and OSINT — piecing together an answer from information deliberately left in plain sight around the room.

The format works because it inverts how security is usually taught. Instead of being told that a weak password is dangerous, a team spends four minutes of a countdown proving it. Nothing in the room needs prior experience — the difficulty curve is built so the first lock teaches you the skill the third lock will test.

Cryptography locks

Caesar, Vigenère, Base64 and hex — decode to get the combination.

Steganography

Payloads hidden inside images, audio and file metadata.

Forensics & logs

Read the trail an intruder left behind and name what they did.

OSINT

Assemble the final key from clues scattered in open view.

EVENT 03

Kastral Tech Fest

6 events Open to all branches

The broad-tent fest on the CYZERA calendar. Kastral deliberately mixes strategy, gaming and creative categories so that the fest is not limited to people who already identify as "technical" — the photographer, the chess player and the gamer all have a bracket of their own.

Entries are open to all branches and years, and members may register for more than one event as long as the schedules allow.

Chess

Knockout board play — pure strategy, no assistance permitted.

Ludo

The high-noise, high-fun bracket. Luck opens the door, tactics close it.

PES

Head-to-head football fixtures played out as a seeded tournament.

Videography

Shoot and cut to a theme. Judged on story, framing and edit discipline.

Photography

A single frame against the brief — composition, light and intent.

Human Firewall

The people-side of security. Teams face social engineering scenarios — phishing mails, pretext phone calls, fake login pages and baiting attempts — and score by spotting the manipulation before they fall for it.

How this is protected

Logging in happens on the server. The password is checked against a salted scrypt hash in cyzera.db, and a successful login returns an HttpOnly cookie the page itself cannot read — so a visitor cannot fake a session by editing storage in devtools. Every change to the registration links is re-checked server-side, and repeated failed logins from one address are locked out for 15 minutes. Locked out of the account entirely? Run py -3 server.py --reset-password cyzera_admin on the machine hosting the site.
